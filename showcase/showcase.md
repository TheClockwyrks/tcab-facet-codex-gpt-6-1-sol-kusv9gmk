# Facet

A quiet board. Seven kinds of cut stone. One swap that changes everything. Take hold of a gem, offer it to a neighbor, and release to shatter a line of three or more. There is no timer: find the move you want to make.

Every clear leaves pressure behind. The stones beside it fracture, then become flawed. Touch a flawed stone with another clear and it shatters too, scoring double and passing the reaction along. Build a fault line, then choose when to spend it.

Longer lines leave rarer cuts. Four create a brilliant that takes its surrounding ring; crossing lines create a star that takes its row and column. Five or more create a colorless prism. Trade it against any stone to clear that entire kind.

Fresh stones fall into the gaps, and each new step of a chain is worth more. Reach the level’s target, admire your longest chain and best move, then continue with your score intact. Keep going until the board has no moves left.
